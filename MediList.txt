SAFETY NOTES:
- Avoid Aspirin in children (risk of Reye’s syndrome).
- Many cough syrups are not recommended under age 6.
- NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Naproxen, Diclofenac) can irritate stomach/kidneys if misused.
- Antibiotics (Azithromycin, Amoxicillin) should only be taken when prescribed.
